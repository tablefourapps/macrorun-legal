Restore Plus on iPhone

Open MacroRun, go to the Plus screen, and tap Restore Purchases.

Cancel or manage a subscription

On iPhone: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.

On Android: Google Play > Subscriptions.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Erase local app data

In the app, open Privacy, then Erase data if that option is present. Otherwise, deleting the app removes data stored on the device.
