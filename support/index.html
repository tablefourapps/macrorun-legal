Restore Plus

Open MacroRun, go to the Plus screen, and tap Restore Purchases. On iPhone, use the same Apple ID you subscribed with. On Android, use the same Google account you subscribed with in Google Play.

Cancel or manage a subscription

On iPhone: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.

On iPhone, cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

On Android: Google Play > Subscriptions.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Erase your data

Your profile, meal log, workouts, saved foods, and achievements stay on your device. MacroRun has no sign-in, so there is no account to delete. To delete this data, clear MacroRun's data on your device or delete the app. Because we don't hold this data, we can't recover it for you if it is deleted.

Photo estimates: turn them off in Profile → Photo estimates → Turn off.

Usage analytics: turn them off in You → Privacy & data. Email hello@tablefourapps.com if you also want past events deleted.

Scan counter: our server keeps a scan counter (a random device ID, a salted hash of the IP address, and counts) for the rolling scan limit, and deletes it within 90 days after your device's last scan.

To ask what personal information we hold, or to have it deleted, email hello@tablefourapps.com with the subject "Privacy request". See Your rights.
