MacroRun Terms

Published by TableFour LLC for MacroRun.

What MacroRun is

MacroRun is a leftover-macro tracker. You log meals, see what protein, carbs, fat, and kcal remain today, and order nearby dishes that actually fit those leftovers.

Estimates, not medical advice

Suggested fridge meals, describe estimates, and catalog macros are estimates, not lab values and not medical advice. You review and edit before save.

Orders leave the app

Order opens DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, or Maps (pickup, including In-N-Out) in the system browser or a new tab. We do not take payment for restaurant orders. Those services have their own terms.

Photos and location

Leftover photos, plated-meal photos, and typed meal descriptions you choose to send go to our estimator (xAI Grok) after you allow it. Location is optional: when you open or refresh Nearby, we send that lat/lng through our server to Google Places to list restaurants. Profile data stays on-device.

MacroRun Plus

Plus is an auto-renewing subscription. It gives you 50 successful photo scans per 30 days (Free: 3 per 7 days) and all 16 Train programs (2 starter programs stay free). Everything else in MacroRun, including Fridge (its photo scans count toward your scan limit), Nearby, the diary, food search, barcode scanning, How-to demos, and CSV export, stays free. You pay the price and billing period shown in the App Store or Google Play before you confirm. There is no free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID (iPhone) or Google Play account (Android) when you confirm the purchase. Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period at the then-current price until you cancel. On iPhone, cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions; Apple charges the renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. On Android, cancel before the renewal date in Google Play > Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals, and you keep Plus until the end of the period you already paid for. Payments and refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies, except where the law gives you other rights. If the price changes, Apple or Google will notify you in advance as required and, where required, ask you to agree before your subscription renews at the new price.

Exercise and training

Train programs and How-to demos are general fitness information, not medical advice, diagnosis, physical therapy, or personal training. Exercise can cause injury. Talk to a doctor before you start or change a workout, especially if you have a medical condition, pain, or are returning from injury. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, or shortness of breath, and seek medical care when needed. You use Train at your own risk.

Some How-to buttons open exercise pages on other sites (for example MuscleWiki or ExRx) in your browser. Those sites own their content and terms. MacroRun does not control whether a link works, what they show, or their availability. A missing or broken How-to is not a MacroRun form video. We do not sell or rehost their videos in this build.
