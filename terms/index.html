What MacroRun is

MacroRun is a leftover-macro tracker. You log meals, see what protein, carbs, fat, and kcal remain today, and order nearby dishes that actually fit those leftovers.

If you are under the age of majority where you live (18 in most US states, Australia, and New Zealand), you may use MacroRun only with the involvement of a parent or guardian, who agrees to these Terms for you and is responsible for any purchases made on your device.

Estimates, not medical advice

Protein, carbs, fat, and calorie figures, including photo and typed-meal estimates, fridge meal suggestions, and restaurant catalog macros, are estimates (4×P + 4×C + 9×F), not lab values. They can be wrong. Review and edit them before you save. MacroRun is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it is not a dietitian. Don't rely on it to manage a medical condition (for example diabetes, food allergies, kidney disease, pregnancy, or an eating disorder). Talk to a qualified health professional before making significant diet changes.

Orders leave the app

Order opens Maps (pickup, including In-N-Out) in the system browser or a new tab. DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, or a restaurant site open only when we have a verified order URL. We do not take payment for restaurant orders. Those services have their own terms.

Photos and location

Leftover, plated-meal, and fridge photos and typed meal descriptions you choose to send go to our estimator (xAI Grok) after you allow it. Location is optional: when you open or refresh Nearby, we send your latitude and longitude through our server to Geoapify to list restaurants (map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). Your profile and logs stay on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.
