Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Who we are

Effective date: October 2, 2026
Who we are: TableFour LLC ("TableFour", "we", "us"), the company that makes MacroRun. Email: hello@tablefourapps.com.

MacroRun is currently offered only in the United States, on iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play). It is not currently offered in, or directed to people in, other countries (including the European Union, the UK, and China), and we do not claim compliance with the EU GDPR. The main body of this policy applies to everyone. Extra details for specific places are in the Regional supplements at the end.

1. The short version

  • Sign-in is optional. You can use MacroRun without an account: your profile, meal log, workouts, saved foods, and achievements stay on your device. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address from them (section 3j), and you can delete your account at any time in You → Account → Delete account.
  • Cloud backup is available only while you are signed in. If you sign in, the app can back up your profile, meal log, workouts, saved foods, goals, streaks, and settings to our servers so you can restore them on a new device (section 3k). The backup is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Photo and typed meal estimates are sent through our server to xAI only after you allow it, so we can estimate protein, carbs, fat, and cal. We do not keep the photo.
  • Location is used only when you open or refresh Nearby, and only if you allow it.
  • Our server keeps a small scan counter (a random device ID and a salted hash of your IP address) so the free scan limit works.
  • Usage analytics are optional and off by default. We record them only if you say yes on the analytics consent screen, and you can turn them off at any time.
  • We do not sell your personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2. Information that stays on your device

Your profile, meal log, workouts, saved foods, and achievements are stored locally on your device. We do not receive them — unless you back them up to our servers while signed in (section 3k). Deleting the app, or clearing MacroRun's data on your device, removes them. Because we don't hold this data (other than a backup you chose to make), we can't recover it for you if it is deleted.

Cloud sync is off in this version. The backup in section 3k is a single stored snapshot for restore, not continuous sync. If we ever turn sync on, we will update this policy first and ask for any consent the law requires. Optional sign-in with Apple or Google is available (section 3j); everything else in this section applies whether or not you sign in.

3. Information we collect and why

a. Meal photos and typed meal descriptions (only after you allow it). When you allow it, leftover photos, plated-meal photos, fridge photos, and typed meal descriptions are sent through our server to our estimator (xAI Grok) so we can identify food and estimate protein, carbs, fat, and cal. Fridge photo scans also send your remaining calories, protein, carbs, and fat for today, so the estimator can suggest meals that fit them. We do not keep the photo after the estimate. We keep only a hash of the photo (a fingerprint that can't be turned back into the photo) in server memory, not in our database, for up to 30 days so the same photo is not billed twice. That hash is deleted when it is 30 days old, and sooner if the server process ends. We do not use those photos or descriptions for ads. Empty or unreadable photos are not stored as a meal. You can withdraw that permission in Profile, under Photo estimates, with Turn off. Please avoid including faces, documents, or other personal details in food photos.

b. Camera, photo, and notification access. If you allow camera access (iPhone: the Camera permission; Android: the Camera permission), MacroRun uses the camera to take meal and fridge photos and to scan barcodes. Photos you pick from your library come through the system photo picker, which shares only the photos you choose. Barcode scanning happens on your device. On Android, barcode scanning uses Google's ML Kit, which processes the camera image on the device and does not send it to Google. ML Kit does send Google basic diagnostics and usage metrics (such as device model, app version, performance data, and a per-installation identifier) to maintain and improve ML Kit. On iPhone, barcode scanning uses Apple's built-in camera frameworks on the device. MacroRun does not use the microphone and does not ask for access to your whole photo library. If you allow notifications (iPhone, and Android 13 and later, ask for this permission), MacroRun schedules reminders and streak nudges on your device. These are local notifications: we do not run a push-notification server, and no push token or notification data is sent to us. You can turn notifications off in the app or in your device settings.

c. Scan counter (random device ID and IP address). To enforce the photo-scan limits (Free: 3 successful photo scans per device per 7 days; Plus: 50 per 30 days), our server stores a record with a random device ID created by the app, a salted hash of your IP address at the time of the scan (not the raw IP address), scan counts and times, and whether the Free or Plus limit applied. We use it only to apply the limit and to prevent abuse of the free tier.

d. Location for Nearby (only when you allow it). When you open or refresh Nearby and have allowed location, your device's precise latitude and longitude are sent through our server to Geoapify to list restaurants around you. We then rank them by your food likes, distance, and leftover fit. Restaurant map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). We do not store your coordinates on our server. If you decline location, we do not guess your city. You can turn location off at any time: on iPhone in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > MacroRun, and on Android in Settings > Apps > MacroRun > Permissions > Location.

e. Subscription status. MacroRun Plus is bought through Apple In-App Purchase on iPhone or Google Play Billing on Android. Apple or Google processes the payment. We receive the purchase and entitlement status (for example, whether Plus is active) with a scan request, and we do not receive your card details. We use that status to unlock Plus features and apply the Plus scan limit. We do not store a separate subscription record, purchase token, or transaction ID on our server. On Android, the result of checking your purchase with Google is held briefly in server memory (up to 5 minutes) and is not written to our database.

f. Optional usage analytics (off unless you say yes). MacroRun asks before recording any usage analytics. Analytics are off by default. If you choose "Help improve MacroRun" on the analytics consent screen, we record product events on our own servers (hosted on Neon) to understand how MacroRun is used and improve it. Events cover things like scan attempts, views of the Plus screen, Nearby opens, finished training sessions, and screens viewed, together with a random, pseudonymous analytics ID and a record of your consent choice. Events never include meal photos, your location, your name, or your email address. We keep each event for up to 2 years and then delete it. You can turn analytics off at any time in You → Privacy & data. We then stop recording new events. Email us if you also want past events deleted. We do not use a third-party analytics SDK. We do not sell or share analytics data, and we do not use it for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

g. Bug reports and emails. "Report a bug" opens your mail app addressed to hello@tablefourapps.com. We receive whatever you choose to send, such as your email address, your message, attachments, and the optional app and device version. We use it to reply and fix problems.

h. Basic technical data. Our app and API are hosted on xAI's Grok Build platform, which uses Cloudflare (network edge and security, including bot protection) and Vercel (application hosting) as its infrastructure providers. Like any internet service, these providers process your IP address and basic request details (such as time and the app request made) to deliver and secure the service when the app or our website talks to our server. This data is used to deliver the request, keep the service secure, and debug errors.

i. Security cookie. Cloudflare may set one strictly necessary security cookie, __cf_bm, when our website or the app connects to our server, to detect bots. It is not used for advertising or analytics. Cloudflare says it expires after 30 minutes of continuous inactivity.

j. Optional sign-in with Apple or Google. Sign-in is optional. If you sign in, Apple or Google sends us your name and email address. We use them only to run your account: keeping you signed in, associating Plus status and scan quota with your account, and letting you delete your account. We do not use your name or email for advertising, and we do not share them except as described in section 4. If you use Apple's private email relay, we receive the relay address instead of your real address. You can sign out in You → Account → Sign out, and you can permanently delete your account in You → Account → Delete account.

k. Cloud backup (only while you are signed in). If you are signed in, the app can back up your health profile and stats, diary entries, workouts, saved foods, goals, streaks, and settings to our servers so you can restore them on a new device. Only one snapshot is kept: each backup replaces the previous one. Backups are only created while you are signed in, and deleting your account deletes the backup (section 10). Your name and email address are not part of the backup; they live in your account record (section 3j).

l. Food label and nutrient lookup (USDA FoodData Central). When you search the food database or when we refine the sub-macros (fiber, sugar, saturated fat, trans fat) of a logged meal, the food name, search text, and portion grams you entered are sent through our server to the U.S. Department of Agriculture's FoodData Central (api.nal.usda.gov) to look up the label values. We do not send your name, email address, or account details with these lookups. USDA is a U.S. government service; its privacy policy applies to those lookups.

We do not collect your contacts or advertising identifier (IDFA or Android advertising ID), and we do not use third-party ad or analytics SDKs. We collect your name and email address only if you choose to sign in (section 3j).

5. Estimates, not medical advice

Protein, carbs, fat, and calories are estimates (4×P + 4×C + 9×F). MacroRun is not medical advice, diagnosis, or a dietitian.

6. Third-party How-to sites

If you tap How-to, your device may open MuscleWiki, ExRx, or another allowlisted demo site. That visit is between you and that site, under its privacy policy. MacroRun does not receive the page content from that visit.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards for the kind of information we handle. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the app and our server, access controls on our systems and vendor accounts, and minimising what we collect and keep. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

9. Data breaches

If a data breach affects your personal information, we will investigate promptly, take steps to contain it, and notify you and the relevant regulators when the law requires it.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Photo estimates: turn them off in Profile → Photo estimates → Turn off. If you do, photos and typed descriptions are no longer sent to xAI.
  • Camera, location, and notifications: decline the permission, or turn it off later in your device settings (iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security; Android: Settings > Apps > MacroRun > Permissions). Features that need the permission then stop using it.
  • Usage analytics: they stay off unless you opt in. Turn them off any time in You → Privacy & data.
  • On-device data: delete it by clearing MacroRun's data or deleting the app.
  • Delete your account: You → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account record, sessions, sign-in links, and any cloud backup (section 3k) on our server. Your on-device data (section 2) is not affected — delete the app to remove that.
  • Requests: you can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, give you a copy, correct it, or delete it. Email hello@tablefourapps.com with the subject "Privacy request". If you never signed in, we have no account for you; we may ask for the approximate date and time of your scans and the IP address you used, so we can find a scan-counter record. If you signed in, your email address identifies your account. We won't discriminate against you for using your rights. The regional supplements describe additional rights.

11. Children

MacroRun is for people 13 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Do Not Track

MacroRun does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so there is no cross-site tracking for a Do Not Track signal to turn off. We do not change our practices in response to browser Do Not Track signals.

13. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes here and update the effective date. For material changes, we will also tell you in the app before the change takes effect. We will update this policy, and ask for consent where required, before we turn on any new data collection such as cloud sync, or change what usage analytics include.

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

This section is our consumer health data privacy policy under Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada's consumer health data law (SB 370), Connecticut's consumer health data provisions, and similar laws. It can be linked on its own.

What consumer health data we collect. We treat the following as consumer health data because they may reveal information about your diet, nutrition, or body:

  • meal, fridge, and leftover photos and typed meal descriptions you choose to send for an estimate;
  • the protein, carbs, fat, and cal estimates returned for them;
  • for fridge scans, your remaining calories, protein, carbs, and fat for today.

Your profile, goals, meal log, and workouts stay on your device and are not collected by us — unless you back them up while signed in (section 3k). In that case your backup on our servers also contains them, is kept while your account exists, and is deleted when you delete your account. We do not use your location to infer anything about your health, and we do not store it.

Where it comes from. Directly from you, when you take or pick a photo or type a description, and from our estimator's response.

Why we collect and use it. Only to give you the food estimate or meal suggestion you asked for, and (for the photo hash) to avoid billing the same photo twice. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Consent. We collect this data only after you allow photo estimates in the app. You can withdraw consent at any time in Profile → Photo estimates → Turn off. After that, we stop sending new photos or descriptions.

Who we share it with. Only processors acting on our behalf: our hosting providers, xAI's Grok Build platform with Cloudflare and Vercel (which host our server and relay the request), xAI's Grok API (which produces the estimate), and USDA FoodData Central (which looks up label values for food names, search text, and portions you enter; see section 4). We do not share consumer health data with any other third party or affiliate, and we do not sell it.

How long we keep it. We do not store the photo or description after the estimate is returned. The photo hash is kept in server memory for up to 30 days, then deleted, and it is not stored in our database. The estimate itself is saved only on your device, if you choose to log it — unless you use cloud backup while signed in, in which case logged estimates are also in your backup, kept while your account exists and deleted when you delete your account (section 3k).

Your rights. Subject to applicable law, you can:

  • confirm whether we collect, share, or sell your consumer health data, and get a copy of it;
  • get a list of the third parties and affiliates we have shared it with (currently xAI, Cloudflare, Vercel, and USDA FoodData Central), with their contact details on request;
  • ask us to delete it (we will also instruct our processors to delete it);
  • withdraw your consent.

Because we don't keep photos or descriptions after the estimate, in most cases we will hold no consumer health data about you by the time you ask — unless you have a cloud backup (section 3k), which we delete on request or when you delete your account. We will tell you so.

How to make a request. Email hello@tablefourapps.com with the subject "Consumer health data request". We will respond within 45 days. Where the law allows, we may extend this by another 45 days if reasonably necessary, and we will tell you if we do. We may ask for information to reasonably verify your request.

Appeals. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying with the subject "Consumer health data appeal" within 45 days of our decision. We will respond in writing within 45 days, explaining what we did and why. If your appeal is denied, you may contact your state Attorney General: Washington (atg.wa.gov), Nevada (ag.nv.gov), or Connecticut (portal.ct.gov/ag).
