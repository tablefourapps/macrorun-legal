MacroRun Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026
Who we are: TableFour LLC ("TableFour", "we", "us"), the company that makes MacroRun. Mailing address: 2108 N St Ste N, Sacramento, CA 95816. Email: hello@tablefourapps.com.

MacroRun is currently offered only in the United States, Australia, and New Zealand. It is not currently offered in, or directed to people in, other countries (including the European Union, the UK, and China), and we do not claim compliance with the EU GDPR. The main body of this policy applies to everyone. Extra details for specific places are in the Regional supplements at the end.

1. The short version

  • No account needed. MacroRun 1.0 has no sign-in. Your profile, meal log, workouts, saved foods, and achievements stay on your device.
  • Photo and typed meal estimates are sent through our server to xAI only after you allow it, so we can estimate protein, carbs, fat, and kcal. We do not keep the photo.
  • Location is used only when you open or refresh Nearby, and only if you allow it.
  • Our server keeps a small scan counter (a random device ID and your IP address) so the free scan limit works.
  • We do not sell your personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2. Information that stays on your device

Your profile, meal log, workouts, saved foods, and achievements are stored locally on your device. We do not receive them. Deleting the app, or clearing MacroRun's data on your device, removes them. Because we don't hold this data, we can't recover it for you if it is deleted.

Cloud sync and analytics are off in this version. If we ever turn on cloud sync, analytics, or accounts, we will update this policy first and ask for any consent the law requires.

3. Information we collect and why

a. Meal photos and typed meal descriptions (only after you allow it). When you allow it, leftover photos, plated-meal photos, fridge photos, and typed meal descriptions are sent through our server to our estimator (xAI Grok) so we can identify food and estimate protein, carbs, fat, and kcal. Fridge photo scans also send your remaining calories, protein, carbs, and fat for today, so the estimator can suggest meals that fit them. We do not keep the photo after the estimate. We keep only a short-lived hash (a fingerprint that can't be turned back into the photo) so the same photo is not billed twice. We do not use those photos or descriptions for ads. Empty or unreadable photos are not stored as a meal. You can withdraw that permission in Profile, under Photo estimates, with Turn off. Please avoid including faces, documents, or other personal details in food photos.

b. Scan counter (random device ID and IP address). To enforce the photo-scan limits (Free: 3 successful photo scans per device per 7 days; Plus: 50 per 30 days), our server stores a record with a random device ID created by the app, your device's IP address at the time of the scan, and scan counts and times. We use it only to apply the limit and to prevent abuse of the free tier.

c. Location for Nearby (only when you allow it). When you open or refresh Nearby and have allowed location, your device's precise latitude and longitude are sent through our server to Geoapify to list restaurants around you. We then rank them by your food likes, distance, and leftover fit. Restaurant map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). We do not store your coordinates on our server. If you decline location, we do not guess your city. You can turn location off at any time in iOS Settings.

d. Subscription status. MacroRun Plus is bought through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment. We receive the purchase and entitlement status (for example, whether Plus is active), and we do not receive your card details. We use that status to unlock Plus features and apply the Plus scan limit.

e. Bug reports and emails. "Report a bug" opens your mail app addressed to hello@tablefourapps.com. We receive whatever you choose to send, such as your email address, your message, attachments, and the optional app and device version. We use it to reply and fix problems.

f. Basic technical data. Like any internet service, our hosting provider (Vercel) receives your IP address and basic request details (such as time and the app request made) when the app talks to our server. This data is used to deliver the request, keep the service secure, and debug errors.

We do not collect your name, contacts, or advertising identifier, and we do not use third-party ad or analytics SDKs.

5. Estimates, not medical advice

Protein, carbs, fat, and calories are estimates (4×P + 4×C + 9×F). MacroRun is not medical advice, diagnosis, or a dietitian.

6. Third-party How-to sites

If you tap How-to, your device may open MuscleWiki, ExRx, or another allowlisted demo site. That visit is between you and that site, under its privacy policy. MacroRun does not receive the page content from that visit.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards for the kind of information we handle. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the app and our server, access controls on our systems and vendor accounts, and minimising what we collect and keep. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

9. Data breaches

If a data breach affects your personal information, we will investigate promptly, take steps to contain it, and notify you and the relevant regulators when the law requires it. See the regional supplements for Australia and New Zealand.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Photo estimates: turn them off in Profile → Photo estimates → Turn off. If you do, photos and typed descriptions are no longer sent to xAI.
  • Location: decline, or turn it off in iOS Settings. Nearby then won't use your location.
  • On-device data: delete it by clearing MacroRun's data or deleting the app.
  • Requests: you can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, give you a copy, correct it, or delete it. Email hello@tablefourapps.com with the subject "Privacy request". Because we have no accounts, we may ask for the approximate date and time of your scans and the IP address you used, so we can find a scan-counter record. We won't discriminate against you for using your rights. The regional supplements describe additional rights.

11. Children

MacroRun is for people 13 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Do Not Track

MacroRun does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so there is no cross-site tracking for a Do Not Track signal to turn off. We do not change our practices in response to browser Do Not Track signals.

13. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes here and update the effective date. For material changes, we will also tell you in the app before the change takes effect. We will update this policy, and ask for consent where required, before we turn on any new data collection such as cloud sync or analytics.

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

This section is our consumer health data privacy policy under Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada's consumer health data law (SB 370), Connecticut's consumer health data provisions, and similar laws. It can be linked on its own.

What consumer health data we collect. We treat the following as consumer health data because they may reveal information about your diet, nutrition, or body: - meal, fridge, and leftover photos and typed meal descriptions you choose to send for an estimate; - the protein, carbs, fat, and kcal estimates returned for them; - for fridge scans, your remaining calories, protein, carbs, and fat for today.

Your profile, goals, meal log, and workouts stay on your device and are not collected by us. We do not use your location to infer anything about your health, and we do not store it.

Where it comes from. Directly from you, when you take or pick a photo or type a description, and from our estimator's response.

Why we collect and use it. Only to give you the food estimate or meal suggestion you asked for, and (for the photo hash) to avoid billing the same photo twice. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Consent. We collect this data only after you allow photo estimates in the app. You can withdraw consent at any time in Profile → Photo estimates → Turn off. After that, we stop sending new photos or descriptions.

Who we share it with. Only processors acting on our behalf: Vercel (our server, which relays the request) and xAI (which produces the estimate). See section 4 for what xAI keeps and for how long. We do not share consumer health data with any other third party or affiliate, and we do not sell it.

How long we keep it. We do not store the photo or description after the estimate is returned. The short-lived photo hash is deleted shortly after. The estimate itself is saved only on your device, if you choose to log it.

Your rights. Subject to applicable law, you can: - confirm whether we collect, share, or sell your consumer health data, and get a copy of it; - get a list of the third parties and affiliates we have shared it with (currently Vercel and xAI), with their contact details on request; - ask us to delete it (we will also instruct our processors to delete it); - withdraw your consent.

Because we don't keep photos or descriptions after the estimate, in most cases we will hold no consumer health data about you by the time you ask. We will tell you so.

How to make a request. Email hello@tablefourapps.com with the subject "Consumer health data request". We will respond within 45 days. Where the law allows, we may extend this by another 45 days if reasonably necessary, and we will tell you if we do. We may ask for information to reasonably verify your request.

Appeals. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying with the subject "Consumer health data appeal" within 45 days of our decision. We will respond in writing within 45 days, explaining what we did and why. If your appeal is denied, you may contact your state Attorney General: Washington (atg.wa.gov), Nevada (ag.nv.gov), or Connecticut (portal.ct.gov/ag).
