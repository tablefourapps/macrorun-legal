Log meals. Stay on your macros. Train with a plan.

MacroRun is a mobile app from TableFour LLC that helps you track food, estimate protein, carbs, fat, and calories, find leftover-friendly places nearby, and follow simple workout programs.

Macro and calorie numbers are estimates, not medical advice, diagnosis, or a dietitian service.

What MacroRun does

Scan Food & Fridge

Snap a plated meal or fridge leftovers. MacroRun uses an AI estimate (xAI Grok) so you can review items, edit macros, and log what you ate.

Nearby

See restaurants near you with leftover-friendly suggested builds when we have verified chain nutrition, or honest tips when we do not.

Log & search

Search common foods and brand items, add meals to your diary, and adjust logged macros anytime.

Train

Browse programs and continue the workout you are following — training lives in the app alongside your food day.
